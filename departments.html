Our Medical Departments

Explore our specialized medical departments, where experienced healthcare professionals provide comprehensive care using modern medical technology and patient-centered approaches.

Explore Our Departments

  1. Cardiology

    Our Cardiology Department provides specialized care for heart and cardiovascular conditions. Our medical team offers diagnosis, treatment, and follow-up care for patients with different heart-related needs.

    Services:

    • Heart Consultation
    • Echocardiography
    • Blood Pressure Monitoring
    • Cardiac Testing
    • ECG
    Available: Sunday – Thursday

  2. Neurology

    The Neurology Department provides specialized diagnosis and treatment for conditions affecting the brain, spinal cord, nerves, and nervous system.

    Services:

    • Neurological Consultation
    • Headache Evaluation
    • Nerve Testing
    • Neurological Assessment
    • Follow-up Care
    Available: Sunday – Thursday

  3. Pediatrics

    Our Pediatrics Department provides comprehensive healthcare for infants, children, and teenagers in a safe and comfortable environment.

    Services:

    • Pediatric Consultation
    • Child Health Checkups
    • Vaccination Services
    • Growth Monitoring
    • Pediatric Emergency Care
    Available: Saturday – Wednesday

  4. Orthopedics

    The Orthopedics Department specializes in the diagnosis and treatment of conditions affecting bones, joints, muscles, and movement.

    Services:

    • Orthopedic Consultation
    • Joint Care
    • Sports Injury Treatment
    • Fracture Care
    • Rehabilitation Planning
    Available: Monday – Friday

  5. Dermatology

    Our Dermatology Department provides medical care for skin, hair, and nail conditions and offers personalized treatment plans for patients.

    Services:

    • Skin Consultation
    • Acne Treatment
    • Hair and Scalp Care
    • Skin Disease Diagnosis
    • Follow-up Care
    Available: Saturday – Wednesday

  6. Dentistry

    The Dentistry Department provides comprehensive oral and dental care, focusing on prevention, diagnosis, and treatment of dental conditions.

    Services:

    • Dental Checkups
    • Teeth Cleaning
    • Dental Fillings
    • Gum Care
    • Preventive Dentistry
    Available: Sunday – Thursday

  7. Ophthalmology

    Our Ophthalmology Department provides comprehensive eye care, including vision assessment, diagnosis, and treatment of various eye conditions.

    Services:

    • Eye Examination
    • Vision Testing
    • Eye Disease Diagnosis
    • Cataract Evaluation
    • Follow-up Care
    Available: Sunday – Thursday

  8. Ear, Nose and Throat

    The ENT Department specializes in the diagnosis and treatment of conditions affecting the ear, nose, throat, and related areas.

    Services:

    • Ear Examination
    • Hearing Assessment
    • Sinus Care
    • Throat Examination
    • ENT Consultation
    Available: Monday – Friday

  9. Urology

    Our Urology Department provides specialized medical care for conditions affecting the urinary system and related health concerns.

    Services:

    • Urological Consultation
    • Urinary Tract Evaluation
    • Kidney Stone Assessment
    • Prostate Evaluation
    • Follow-up Care
    Available: Sunday – Thursday

  10. Obstetrics & Gynecology

    Our Obstetrics and Gynecology Department provides comprehensive healthcare for women, including routine care, pregnancy services, and gynecological consultations.

    Services:

    • Women's Health Consultation
    • Pregnancy Follow-up
    • Prenatal Care
    • Gynecological Examination
    • Family Planning Consultation
    Available: Saturday – Wednesday

  11. Oncology

    The Oncology Department provides specialized cancer care through coordinated medical services, personalized treatment planning, and continuous patient support.

    Services:

    • Oncology Consultation
    • Cancer Screening
    • Treatment Planning
    • Follow-up Care
    • Supportive Care
    Available: Sunday – Thursday

  12. General Surgery

    Our General Surgery Department provides surgical consultations and comprehensive care before, during, and after a wide range of surgical procedures.

    Services:

    • Surgical Consultation
    • Preoperative Assessment
    • General Surgical Procedures
    • Postoperative Follow-up
    • Wound Care
    Available: Sunday – Thursday
