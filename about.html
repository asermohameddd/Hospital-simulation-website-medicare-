About MediCare Hospital

  • Learn more about our hospital, our journey, and our commitment to providing high-quality healthcare with compassion, safety, and professionalism.

Our Story

  • MediCare Hospital was founded with a clear goal: to create a trusted healthcare environment where patients can receive high-quality medical care with comfort, respect, and compassion.
  • Since our beginning, we have continued to grow by bringing together experienced doctors, skilled nurses, and dedicated healthcare professionals. We have also expanded our medical services and invested in modern facilities and medical technology.
  • Today, MediCare Hospital provides comprehensive healthcare services for individuals and families across our community. Our commitment remains the same: to put patients first and continuously improve the quality of care we provide.

Our Mission

  • Our mission is to provide safe, accessible, and high-quality healthcare to every patient. We are committed to combining medical expertise, modern technology, and compassionate care while treating every patient with dignity, respect, and understanding.

Our Vision

  • Our vision is to become a trusted healthcare institution recognized for medical excellence, patient safety, innovation, and outstanding service. We aim to continuously improve our services and contribute to a healthier community.

Our Values

  • Compassion

    We treat every patient with kindness, empathy, and respect.

  • Patient Safety

  • Excellence

  • Integrity

  • Teamwork

Our history

  • 2011 — The Beginning

    New medical departments were introduced to provide patients with a wider range of specialized healthcare services.

  • 2017 — Advanced Technology

  • 2020 — Emergency Services Expansion

    Our emergency department was expanded to improve access to urgent medical care and support the growing needs of our community.

  • 2023 — New Healthcare Services

    New specialized services were introduced as part of our continued commitment to comprehensive patient care.

  • 2026 — Looking Ahead

    MediCare Hospital continues to develop its facilities, services, and medical team while working toward a healthier future for the community.
