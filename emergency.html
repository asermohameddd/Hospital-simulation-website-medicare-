What To Do In An Emergency

  1. Stay Calm

    Try to remain calm and assess the situation.

  2. Call Emergency Services

    Contact our emergency team or your local emergency service if immediate assistance is required.

  3. Do Not Delay

    Seek professional medical attention as soon as possible.

  4. Follow Medical Instructions

    Follow the instructions provided by emergency medical professionals.

  5. Bring Important Information

    If possible, provide information about medications, allergies, and existing medical conditions.

Important Instructions

  1. Emergency situations require immediate professional medical attention. Do not attempt to diagnose or treat a serious condition yourself.
  2. If the patient is unconscious, having difficulty breathing, experiencing severe bleeding, or showing other serious symptoms, seek emergency medical help immediately.
